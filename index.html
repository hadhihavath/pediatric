Our Vision & Mission

Redefining child healthcare across India

We believe every child—from every corner of India—deserves specialized, affordable, and compassionate medical care delivered in an environment built just for them.

  • Specialized, affordable care for children from newborns to 12 years
  • Child-friendly hospital environments designed to reduce fear and discomfort
  • Modern technology integrated with deeply personalized care
  • Nationwide online consultations and product delivery for rural access
  • Scalable chain model — expanding to urban and semi-urban regions
Our Departments

under one roof

General & Medical Department

Comprehensive pediatric consultations by specialists who understand the unique physiological needs of children. From common infections to complex neurological conditions, every child gets expert attention.

Pediatric Consultations Allergy Treatment Epilepsy Care Infection Management Optometry Speech & Hearing Growth Monitoring Developmental Assessment

Pediatric Dental Department

A child's first dental experience shapes their relationship with oral health for life. Our child-first dental environment turns every visit into a positive, engaging experience with specialists trained in pediatric dentistry.

Pediatric Dental Care Preventive Treatment Early Alignment Corrective Procedures Cavity Prevention Fluoride Treatment

Orthopedic Department

Early detection and correction of musculoskeletal issues in children can prevent lifelong complications. Our orthopedic specialists use the latest diagnostic tools to ensure healthy growth and mobility.

Bone Issue Diagnosis Growth Corrections Posture Assessment Joint Care Fracture Management Mobility Support

Digital Health & Online Services

Bringing pediatric expertise to every home in India. Our digital layer includes video consultations, follow-up tracking, product delivery, and a transparent management system for all stakeholders.

Online Consultations All-India Delivery Diet Plans Skin & Body Care Medical Accessories Follow-up Tracking
Business Model

Revenue & doctor
partnership structures

Three flexible models designed to attract top specialists while ensuring sustainable hospital growth.

Salary-Based Model

Fixed monthly salary for general doctors, ensuring financial stability and consistent patient care quality.

Stable

Revenue Share Model

Experienced specialists receive full consultation fees. The hospital takes a pre-agreed percentage cut, rewarding performance.

High yield

Dental Commission Model

BDS doctors earn on a commission basis tied to treatments performed, creating direct incentive for quality care.

Flexible
100%

Ownership Structure

Centralized control,
shared investment

The central medical company retains 100% legal, operational, and strategic authority. Local investors contribute capital and share in financial returns — operations are never delegated.

Local Investors (capital contribution) 50–60%
Medical Company (operations + ownership) 40–50%
Investors may participate in multiple branches
